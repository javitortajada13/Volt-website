FAQ

Questions

How long does delivery take?

In Bangkok we usually deliver within 1–2 days of confirming your order, free of charge. Elsewhere in Thailand allow 2–4 working days. For Singapore, Malaysia and Vietnam, we'll quote shipping cost and timing on WhatsApp before you pay.

Are the rackets authentic?

Yes. We are the exclusive distributor of Volt Padel in Thailand and Southeast Asia, supplied directly by the brand. Every racket is new, original and comes with its premium cover and protector.

Can I try a racket before buying?

Yes. Demo rackets are available at Sterling Padel Bangkok, Sukhumvit Soi 24. Message us on WhatsApp to arrange a time to test them on court.

How do I pay?

There's no online payment. Once we confirm your order on WhatsApp, we'll send you payment details (bank transfer or PromptPay). Pay when you're ready, and we arrange delivery.

I'm a coach. Can I work with Volt?

We're launching a coach program soon. Coaches will get a personal referral code and earn commission on sales made through it. Register your interest and we'll be in touch.
