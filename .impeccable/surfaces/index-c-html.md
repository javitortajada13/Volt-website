# Surface brief: index-c.html (third direction, for comparison with index.html and index-b.html)

Scope: full landing page. Mode: Persuade. Code-led (no image generation available).
Audience/job: padel players in Bangkok, mostly on phones, choosing between three Volt v5 rackets. Action: one WhatsApp tap with the racket named.
Constraints: light page only (brand pin), prices in THB only, WhatsApp only, official v5 data only, no invented claims, zero em/en dashes (Taste).
Learned from A/B: rackets must stay the hero on mobile; price and trust in the first viewport; the two-question finder is valuable; the concept must not compete with the product.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every Volt is shown as a technical drawing sheet: real spec data drawn as numbered balloons, a parts list and a title block that doubles as the buy box. It refuses the category default of lifestyle hero plus three equal product cards.
OWN-WORLD: cool drawing-paper white, ink black, Volt yellow as the highlighter on the one value that matters (price, selected state). 1px ink hairlines, leader lines with dots, circled item numbers, ruled title block, square corners everywhere. DIN-style condensed lettering (Barlow Condensed standing in for Volt's DIN) for labels and numerals, tabular figures.
STORY: The visitor sees all three rackets and their prices at once on a balance axis, taps one (or answers two questions), reads its sheet, and orders it on WhatsApp from the title block or the sticky order bar.
FIRST VIEWPORT: phone: nav; "Choose your voltage." in two lines; one-line trust sentence; the three rackets side by side on a low-to-high balance axis with model and price under each; yellow "Message Javi" and outline "Help me choose" visible without scrolling. Desktop: headline, sentence and CTAs left; the lineup large on the right.
FORM: candidate 3 of 7 (technical drawing sheet); seed key d79ac948. Raises: from the blacksmith forge (declined), one racket per full-width sheet row with tabular numerals, never three equal cards; from the particle detector (declined), the finder isolates the matching sheet and dims the rest; from the cracktro scroller (declined), nothing enclosed in boxes except the title block; from the noir one-sheet (declined), two-ink discipline, ink plus Volt yellow only; from the gravity garden (declined), one legend held level: the same four item numbers mean the same part on every sheet; from the fansite shrine (declined), a single devoted column in Javi's own order.
Signature interaction: leader lines draw from each balloon to its part the first time a sheet enters view; choosing a racket in the finder dims the other sheets; on phones a bottom order bar follows the sheet in view.
